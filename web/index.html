Photograph one fridge or pantry shelf.

A vision model lists what it sees; OpenCV checks the photo and the reading, re-reads small labels up close, and decides whether to file the list, ask you, or ask for a new photo. You confirm before anything is kept.

Before you upload

  • Your photo is sent to this engine on AWS and to Anthropic's Claude model to be read.
  • This page does not store photos; the engine logs only counts and timings.
  • Leave people, documents and anything personal out of the frame.
