Glass Pantry: your fridge, from photos

Pick your fridge, trace each compartment over a photo, then photograph each shelf, drawer or door bin to fill it. OpenCV straightens the photo, suggests corners and shelf edges, and checks every reading; you confirm everything.

Before you start

Photos go to this engine on AWS, and shelf photos also to Anthropic's Claude model, to be read. Nothing is stored on the server; your fridge is kept in this browser tab only. Leave people, documents and anything personal out of the frame.
