Glass Pantry: your fridge, from photos

Pick your fridge, trace each compartment over a photo, then photograph each shelf, drawer or door bin to fill it. You place the corners and shelf lines by hand; OpenCV straightens the opening and checks every shelf reading; you confirm everything.

Before you start

Photos go to this engine on AWS, and shelf and fridge photos also to Anthropic's Claude model, to be read. Nothing is stored on the server; your fridge is kept in this browser tab only. Leave people, documents and anything personal out of the frame.
